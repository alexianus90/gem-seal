How to play

Drag a tile to shift its row or column. Seal eight matching tiles around a gem.

Gold gems clear normally. From level 6, glowing mint hexagons add 2 moves. From level 21, glowing blue crystals add 3 moves. From level 51, glowing pink stars add 4 moves.

A glowing gem has one mismatched tile around it. Silver-set gems stay anchored while their row and column slide past them.

Undo keeps your previous swipes in the move count.
